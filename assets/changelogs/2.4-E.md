## 🌟 Ankimon v2.4-E 🌟

A huge thank you to [@JadonKJones](https://github.com/JadonKJones) (sakamotosan), [@Nut2026](https://github.com/Nut2026) (Nuz), [@h0tp-ftw](https://github.com/h0tp-ftw) (h0tp), [@scotej](https://github.com/scotej) (Scoot) for their contributions to this update! <3
